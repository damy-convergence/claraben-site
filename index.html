Each participant gets a video built from the plans they actually elected, narrated and rendered for them. Not a generic overview.

Delivered automatically

How it works

  1. 1

    Connect your book of business

    Bring in your companies, plans, and participants from Employee Navigator, or upload plan documents and a census file.

  2. 2

    Approve the plan scripts and deck

    Claraben drafts the scripts from the plan details. You review them, test the answers, and approve the deck.

  3. 3

    Claraben emails each employee their video on schedule

    Every employee gets a personalized video by email, monthly, every other month, or quarterly, and you see who watched.
