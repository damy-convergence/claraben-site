Who benefits

Clear benefits for everyone in the plan

Claraben sends every employee a personalized video about their own benefits, and gives each employer a co-branded plan deck. Here's what that means for each person involved.

BrokerHR DirectorParticipantCarrier

For brokers

Stand out, and stay in front of every client all year

  • Your brand, all year. Your agency's name and logo sit beside the employer's on every video, deck, and email, not just at renewal.
  • A reason to stay. Every employee hears from you on the schedule you pick, monthly, every other month, or quarterly, without extra work for your team.
  • Something to set you apart. Offer small groups a personal benefits video for every employee and a ready-made enrollment deck.
  • You stay in control. Run 100 questions against a plan, check the answers, and approve the scripts before anything goes out.

For HR directors

Less time answering benefits questions, and an easier open enrollment

  • Fewer repeat questions. Employees get plain-language explanations of their own plans, so you can spend less time answering the same questions.
  • Open enrollment, ready to share. A co-branded slide deck with an overview video that you can send to the team or use in a meeting.
  • Nothing to build or send. Claraben emails each employee on the schedule your broker sets.
  • Know who's seen it. See who opened and watched, so you know who might need a follow-up.

For employees

A short video about your own benefits, in plain language

  • About your plan, not a generic one. The video covers the plans you actually chose, with your coverage level, deductible, and out-of-pocket maximum.
  • Plain language. Deductibles, copays, and coinsurance, explained simply in about a minute.
  • On a regular schedule. It arrives by email, so it's there when you need it, on your phone or computer.
  • Helpful reminders. Tips like using the preventive care and cleanings your plan already covers.

For carriers

Better-informed members, with engagement you can see

  • Engagement data. Reports show which members opened and watched their plan videos, by employer and by plan.
  • Members who understand their coverage. Clear explanations of in-network care, preventive care, and costs help members use their plan well.
  • Fewer basic questions. Plain-language explanations up front can mean fewer simple coverage questions reaching your service team.
  • Support for engagement programs. Documented engagement can support a carrier's engagement or discount programs, where they're offered.
